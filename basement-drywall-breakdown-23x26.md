### Step-by-Step Construction Sequence

1. **Step 1: Foam Barrier & Moisture Seal:**
   * Adhere 25 sheets of 1.5″ FOAMULAR XPS directly to clean concrete with PL 300 adhesive.
   * Seal every vertical and horizontal butt joint tightly with 3M 8067 / Tuck Tape to create an unbroken vapor barrier.
2. **Step 2: Snap Lines, Sill Gasket & Plates:**
   * Snap chalk lines 4″ away from foam.
   * Lay closed-cell sill gasket under pressure-treated bottom plates and anchor into the slab with Tapcon screws every 24″–32″.
   * Fasten SPF top plate to joists above.
3. **Step 3: Plumb Studs & Code Fireblocking:**
   * Toe-nail 88 studs at 16″ on-center (measure each board individually; basement floors slope).
   * Install 2×4 horizontal fireblocking every 10′ and seal cavity tops to ceiling joists with orange firebreak foam (IRC R302.11).
4. **Step 4: Drywall Ceiling, Walls & Finish:**
   * Hang 21 ceiling panels first using a rented drywall lift.
   * Hang 27 wall panels horizontally, holding bottom sheets 1/2″ above concrete slab floor.
   * Apply 3 coats of compound (Easy Sand tape coat + 2 Plus 3 skim coats), sand with 150/220 grit pole sander, and apply PVA primer.

### Kansas City Climate Zone 4A/5A Technical Notes

1. **Condensation & Dew Point Physics:**
   * In the KC metro, underground soil temperature remains ~52°F year-round. Summer basement air at 70°F with 65% RH has a dew point of 57°F.
   * Without continuous 1.5″+ XPS rigid foam, humid air permeates wall cavities and condenses against bare 52°F concrete, breeding hidden mold behind drywall.
2. **Beam & Ceiling Soffit Framing:**
   * A 23′ × 26′ span exceeds standard 2×10 / 2×12 lumber limits. The room will feature a central steel I-beam or LVL girder with 1–2 steel lally columns.
   * Frame 2×2 or 2×4 ladder soffits around that girder and main HVAC duct trunks before boarding the ceiling (8 corner beads included in takeoff).
3. **Tool Rentals:**
   * **Rotary Hammer (SDS-Plus):** $20–$25 half-day rental. Punches each 1/4″ Tapcon pilot hole in under 5 seconds (standard cordless drills burn bits and take hours).
   * **Drywall Panel Lift:** $35–$40/day rental. Hoists 21 ceiling sheets (55 lbs each) overhead safely without physical strain.
4. **Turnkey Contractor vs. DIY Economics:**
   * Turnkey general contractor / framing & drywall subcontractor in KC: **$5,500 – $7,200** ($12–$15/LF framing + $2.75–$3.50/sq ft drywall hang/tape).
   * DIY materials ($2,802) + tool rentals ($200) saves **over $3,500**.
